## 1. Project
Build, from scratch, the new official website of the Innovation and Incubation Centre for Entrepreneurship (IICE), a Technology Business Incubator at IISER Bhopal. IICE has 10,000 sq ft in the main building, is supported by NSTEDB, DST (NIDHI-TBI scheme), focuses on bioscience research and entrepreneurship, has a BIRAC Edge page, and incubates faculty/student-led and external startups.

Audiences, in priority order:
1. Prospective incubatees (founders, IISERB students and faculty, external entrepreneurs)
2. Funders, government bodies, industry partners, investors
3. Mentors and ecosystem partners
4. Current startups
5. Job seekers and the general public

Goals: (a) turn visitors into applicants, (b) build credibility fast (numbers, startups, partners), (c) make programs, funding and facilities easy to understand, (d) let staff update events/news/startups without a developer, (e) be fast on mid-range phones and slow networks.

Problems with the old site (a Google Site) to fix:
- Homepage is a wall of text with no stats, startups, events or application CTA.
- Social icons point to generic twitter.com / linkedin.com / instagram.com homepages.
- Typos (e.g. "Mision").
- Apply and contact depend on external Google Forms.
- Navigation is buried (committees are four separate pages).

- Copy-pasted placeholder text: one reference reuses identical blurbs across different funding schemes. Every card needs unique, accurate copy, or an explicit TODO_CONFIRM.

## 4. The tilted card gallery ("card spinbar"), a reusable component
Reference: the "02 / Where it's been tested" section on zaidsayyed.in. Build the same kind of experience from scratch.

Layout and look
- Portrait cards (about 3:4) on a staggered two-slot zig-zag path. Cards in the left slot rotate about -30 degrees (title rises to the right). Cards in the right slot rotate about +30 degrees (title descends to the right). The next cards peek in from the bottom corners.
- The active card is larger, sharp and full colour. It shows a short description, a thin accent line along its bottom edge that doubles as a progress indicator, and a round arrow button (top-right) linking out.
- All other cards are smaller, dimmed and softly blurred. Photo cards are greyscale until active.
- Card anatomy: a tilted pill badge at top-left (e.g. "Incubated", "Graduated", "Grant Won", "Patent"), a bold title at bottom-left running along the tilt. Text-only cards get a dark gradient with a soft radial glow and a faint circular watermark of the badge word. Photo cards use a photo.
- Section header: a numbered eyebrow ("02"), a large title, a muted one-line subline, and a small mono uppercase label at the right with the count, e.g. "STARTUP SPOTLIGHT (06)".

Behaviour
- Scroll-linked: pin the section while scrolling advances the cards along the path. The active card settles into the focus slot, and the others shift one position and re-blur. Use transform/opacity only, with smooth easing.
- Custom cursor on desktop: a small dot that grows into a large circle with an arrow icon over the active card, signalling "open". Hide it on touch devices.
- Keyboard and a11y: cards are real links in DOM order. Focus brings a card to active. Provide visible previous/next buttons and a "Showing 2 of 6" text for non-scroll users. Never trap scroll or focus.
- prefers-reduced-motion: no tilt animation or scroll-pinning, just a simple grid of cards.
- Mobile: reduce tilt to 8 degrees or less, or switch to a horizontal snap carousel. Avoid expensive blur on many layers.
- Performance: 6 to 8 cards max, lazy-load images, avoid layout thrash, and use will-change sparingly.
- Data-driven from /content (badge, title, description, optional image, link).
- Primary use: homepage "Startup Spotlight and Recognitions". The same component can power Milestones on the About page.

## 7. Design direction
Credible, modern, science-meets-startup. It should not look like a generic template or a government PDF portal. Generous whitespace, strong typographic hierarchy, consistent card/grid system.
- Palette: derive it from the IISER Bhopal logo when I supply it. Until then use a deep blue/teal base with one warm accent. Light theme by default, dark mode supported.
- Type: one display face plus one text face, self-hosted, with a Devanagari-capable fallback.
- Motion: purposeful only (section reveals, count-up, spinbar). Always respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Images: use aspect-ratio placeholders now. I will supply real lab, campus and event photos.
- Icons: one consistent set (e.g. Lucide).

## 8. Tech
Defaults (change only with a one-paragraph justification):
- Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS; Framer Motion or GSAP for motion.
- All content lives as typed JSON/MDX in /content (startups, events, news, team, stats, documents, announcements) so non-developers can edit it. Optionally add a Git-based CMS config (Decap or Keystatic).
- Static export, so it can be hosted anywhere, including institute servers.
- Forms post to a configurable endpoint set by an environment variable (Apps Script, Formspree or similar). Include client and server validation, a spam honeypot, success/error states, and no paid SDKs.
- Self-host fonts. Map is a click-to-load embed.

## 9. Quality bars (must pass before you finish)
- Lighthouse mobile: Performance 90+, Accessibility 100, Best Practices and SEO 95+. LCP under 2.5 s, CLS under 0.1.
- Responsive from 360 px to 1920 px, mobile-first. No hero asset above about 500 KB. Use AVIF/WebP, responsive sizes and lazy loading.
- WCAG 2.2 AA: contrast, keyboard navigation, skip link, landmarks, alt text, focus states. Follow GIGW 3.0 (Guidelines for Indian Government Websites and Apps) where practical.
- i18n-ready structure (English default, Hindi addable later).
- SEO: per-page metadata, OpenGraph, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, JSON-LD (Organization, Event).
