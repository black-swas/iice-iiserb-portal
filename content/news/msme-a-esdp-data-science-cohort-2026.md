From 6 to 10 July 2026, IICE collaborated with the **Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (EECS)** at IISER Bhopal to deliver a one-week **Advanced Entrepreneurship and Skill Development Programme (A-ESDP) on Data Science**, sponsored by the ESDP Division of the **Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME), Government of India**.

### Programme Curriculum & Outcomes

- **Applied AI & Data Engineering:** Sessions led by IISER Bhopal faculty—including **Dr. Tanmay Basu**, **Dr. Haroon Lone**, and **Dr. Vinod Kurmi**—covered machine learning pipelines, clinical natural language processing, computer vision, and edge-sensor analytics.
- **Entrepreneurial Translation:** Participants examined how data-science models move from academic benchmarks to regulated commercial products, featuring case studies from campus-incubated ventures such as **Protomate AI** (*Manomitra* multilingual clinical screening) and **Intisen Technology** (smart sensor analytics).
- **Government Support Linkages:** Officials briefed participants on Udyam registration, MSME Innovative Scheme grants, and incubation pathways at IICE.
