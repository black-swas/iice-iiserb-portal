Across 2024 and 2025, IISER Bhopal's Innovation and Incubation Centre for Entrepreneurship (IICE) has seen a wave of research-intensive spin-offs translating peer-reviewed laboratory breakthroughs into incorporated commercial ventures headquartered on the 2nd Floor of the Main Building.

### Research Translation Highlights

1. **Luminojen Pvt Ltd:** Co-founded by **Prof. Apurba Lal Koner**, **Prof. Gobardhan Das**, **Dhananjaya Sahoo**, and **Naveneet Dubey**, Luminojen commercializes wash-free organelle-specific fluorescent probes such as *TD-ER* for super-resolution live-cell imaging and molecular diagnostics.
2. **Xtalsphere Pvt Ltd:** Mentored by **Dr. Subhajit Roychowdhury**, Xtalsphere synthesizes defect-free single crystals of magnetic Weyl semimetals, topological insulators, and 2D van der Waals magnets via Bridgman, Flux, and CVT crystal-growth techniques.
3. **Cytoforge Therapeutics Pvt Ltd:** Operating from Cube 6 at IICE, Cytoforge is advancing a preclinical pipeline of CD70 and dual-targeting CAR-T therapies alongside *in-vivo* CAR-Macrophage immunotherapies for solid tumours.
4. **Protomate AI Pvt Ltd:** Founded by student innovators **Agamdeep Singh** and **Sattwik Kumar Sahu**, Protomate AI developed *Manomitra*, a multilingual conversational AI assistant for structured preliminary mental-health screening.
5. **Plabeltech Pvt Ltd:** Founded by **Prof. Vishal Rai** and **Er. Vikas Rai**, Plabeltech continues to scale its Linchpin Directed Modification (LDM) site-selective protein labelling and homogeneous Antibody-Drug Conjugate (ADC) platforms.
