On 10 and 11 May 2026, the Innovation and Incubation Centre for Entrepreneurship (IICE) at IISER Bhopal, in partnership with **Madhya Pradesh Laghu Udyog Nigam (MPLUN)** under the **Raising and Accelerating MSME Performance (RAMP)** programme of the Ministry of MSME, hosted a two-day capacity-building and incubation exposure workshop.

### Key Sessions

- **Day 1 — Orientation on Technology Incubation & Public Schemes:** **Mr. Rajeev Rajoria** (CEO, IICE) and **Dr. Arijit Kumar De** introduced participants to IICE's 10,000 sq ft incubation infrastructure, intellectual property support, and technical mentoring model.
- **Day 2 — Hands-On Exposure & Founder Dialogues:** Participating entrepreneurs and student innovators toured campus research facilities and engaged with regional technology founders on scaling manufacturing, STEM education hardware (**Miniguru T-Labs**), and energy systems (**Powernet Technology**).
