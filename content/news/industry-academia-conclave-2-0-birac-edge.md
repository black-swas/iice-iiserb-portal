The Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Bhopal and the Innovation and Incubation Centre for Entrepreneurship (IICE) hosted **Industry–Academia Conclave 2.0** on 8 August 2026 in Lecture Hall Complex L5. The full-day conclave brought together more than 350 participants, including corporate R&D leaders, venture investors, national funding agency representatives, faculty inventors, and deep-science founders.

### Key Highlights from the Conclave

- **Inaugural & Leadership Addresses:** **Prof. Gobardhan Das** (Director, IISER Bhopal and Chairman, IICE Managing Committee) outlined the institute's commitment to translating fundamental discoveries in chemistry, biology, quantum materials, and engineering into indigenous commercial technologies. **Prof. Arijit Kumar De** (Dean, Research & Development) and **Dr. Ramya Sunder Raman** (Professor-in-Charge, IICE) highlighted the campus incubation pipeline.
- **Announcement of the BIRAC-EDGE Scheme:** **Dr. Debasis Nayak**, Coordinator of the BIRAC-EDGE Program at IISER Bhopal, formally announced the BIRAC-EDGE initiative to accelerate translational biotechnology and healthcare ventures with dedicated mentorship and wet-lab support.
- **Plenary Perspectives:** Distinguished addresses were delivered by **Prof. Shekhar C. Mande** (Former Director General, CSIR and President, INSA) and **Prof. Ashutosh Sharma** (Former Secretary, Department of Science & Technology, Government of India) on building self-reliant scientific innovation ecosystems.
- **Startup & Technology Exhibition:** Incubated ventures including **Plabeltech**, **Luminojen**, and **Cytoforge Therapeutics** demonstrated campus-born technologies ranging from site-selective antibody-drug conjugation to organelle-specific molecular probes.
