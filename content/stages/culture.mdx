**Culture** is IICE's core resident and associate incubation programme on the 2nd Floor of the Main Building at IISER Bhopal. It serves ventures that have demonstrated a scientific proof of concept and now need research-grade infrastructure, analytical instrumentation, and company-building support to engineer a commercial product.

Many of IISER Bhopal's flagship research spin-offs—spanning organelle-specific molecular probes (**Luminojen**), topological single crystals (**Xtalsphere**), CAR-T and CAR-Macrophage immunotherapies (**Cytoforge Therapeutics**), flexible diagnostic sensors (**Intisen Technology**), and clinical conversational AI (**Protomate AI**)—have built their core products within the **Culture** ecosystem.
