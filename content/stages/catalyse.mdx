**Catalyse** is IICE's pre-incubation stage, inspired by academic entrepreneurship cells and national deep-tech studio models. It is designed for students, doctoral scholars, postdoctoral researchers, and first-time founders who have a promising scientific result or engineering concept and want structured support to test its real-world viability before incorporating a company.

During **Catalyse**, teams work closely with the **Innovation Club at IICE** and IISER Bhopal faculty advisors to de-risk technical assumptions, establish freedom-to-operate on intellectual property, and prepare competitive proposals for national ignition fellowships such as **DST NIDHI-EIR** and **BIRAC BIG / EDGE**.
