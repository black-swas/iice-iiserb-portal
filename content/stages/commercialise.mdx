**Commercialise** is IICE's late-stage incubation and acceleration track for startups that have validated their product in the laboratory and in early customer trials, and are now focused on revenue growth, manufacturing scale-up, and institutional investment.

Ventures in **Commercialise**—such as **Plabeltech** (scaling site-selective antibody bioconjugation and purification resins globally) and **SimDaas Autonomy** (scaling autonomous-driving simulation pipelines backed by MapmyIndia)—benefit from IISER Bhopal's national scientific credibility and industry-academia partnership network.
