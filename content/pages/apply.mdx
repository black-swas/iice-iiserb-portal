IICE gives incubatees co-working space and labs on the IISER Bhopal campus, mentoring, and help with technical, legal, financial and marketing matters. Applications are made online, using the form further down this page.

## Who can apply

1. Current and former faculty of IISER Bhopal.
2. Students and alumni of IISER Bhopal.
3. Universities with an active R&D collaboration with IISER Bhopal.
4. Startups from outside IISER Bhopal.

Applicants in groups 3 and 4 need an IISER Bhopal faculty member as mentor or collaborator.

## Thrust areas

The proposal should fall in one of five thrust areas:

- Healthcare and Bioengineering
- Environment and Agriculture
- Internet of Things and Artificial Intelligence
- Social and Rural Development Technologies
- Computational Sciences and Information Technology

## Getting started

1. Check that you are eligible.
2. Prepare your business plan and your IP details.
3. Submit the application.
4. IICE screens the application.
5. Shortlisted teams pitch to the Technical Evaluation Committee.
6. IICE decides, and accepted teams sign an onboarding agreement.
7. Move in and start your stage.

## What to prepare

- A business plan.
- Details of the intellectual property or technology the company will be based on.
- For technology developed at IISER Bhopal, a copy of the filed or granted patent application.
