## The campus

The IISER Bhopal campus covers about 200 acres. It is an educational and research campus with lecture halls, a well-equipped Central Instrumentation Facility, a library, computer facilities, research laboratories, meeting halls and auditoriums, and residences for the community. IISER Bhopal is an Institute of National Importance, and its prime focus is to integrate science education with research. The institute is also committed to high ethical values and to social and environmental awareness.

IISER Bhopal has centres of excellence such as CREATES, which provides quality science education to undergraduate and postgraduate students, and CS2, which works on academia–industry collaboration and on making science popular among school students through outreach.

## The region

IISER Bhopal is in Bhopal, the capital of Madhya Pradesh, and is surrounded by premier institutions such as the School of Planning and Architecture (SPA), the National Institute of Fashion Technology (NIFT) and the National Institute for Research in Environmental Health (NIREH). An ecosystem of academic, technological and entrepreneurial excellence is emerging in the region, and IICE aims to stimulate the establishment and growth of technology-based startups and other compatible businesses within it.

Bhopal is in the centre of India, which makes it a good base for startups that want to grow across the country. IICE also expects to contribute to job creation and to the economic health of the region.

## Why it matters for incubation

IISER Bhopal is an established institute with strong infrastructure, eminent faculty and motivated students who want to turn in-house technologies and ideas into commercially viable products. IICE at IISER Bhopal is likely to build an entrepreneurial ecosystem on the campus and in its vicinity.
