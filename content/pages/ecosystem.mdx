## The IISER Bhopal Research Campus

The **Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Bhopal** spans a **200-acre green residential campus** at Bhauri, Bhopal. Established by the Ministry of Education, Government of India, as an **Institute of National Importance** under the NITSER Act, IISER Bhopal integrates undergraduate and postgraduate science and engineering education with frontier research across Natural Sciences, Engineering Sciences, Economic Sciences, and Humanities.

Startups incubated at IICE benefit directly from the institute's research ecosystem:

- **Central Instrumentation Facility (CIF):** A state-of-the-art analytical hub housing high-field NMR spectrometers (400 / 500 / 700 MHz), UHPLC-HRMS/Q-TOF mass spectrometers, Single-Crystal and Powder X-Ray Diffractometers (SC-XRD / PXRD), Field-Emission Scanning Electron Microscopes (FESEM), Laser Scanning Confocal Microscopes, and thermal/spectroscopic analyzers.
- **Centres of Excellence:** Specialized interdisciplinary centres including **CREATES** (Centre for Research on Environment and Sustainable Technologies / Science Education) and **CS2** (Centre for Science and Society), which foster industry-academia collaboration and scientific outreach.
- **Academic & Engineering Depth:** Active research groups across Biological Sciences, Chemistry, Physics, Earth and Environmental Sciences, Mathematics, Chemical Engineering, Data Science and Engineering, and Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (EECS) provide domain mentorship to incubatees.

## The Bhopal & Central India Innovation Corridor

Located in **Bhopal**, the capital of Madhya Pradesh, IISER Bhopal sits at the geographical heart of India with direct rail and air connectivity to all major industrial hubs. The campus lies along the Bhauri institutional corridor alongside premier national institutes:

- **School of Planning and Architecture (SPA) Bhopal**
- **National Institute of Fashion Technology (NIFT) Bhopal**
- **ICMR – National Institute for Research in Environmental Health (NIREH)**
- **All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS) Bhopal** and **CSIR-AMPRI Bhopal**

This concentration of national research laboratories, clinical institutions, and industrial estates (Mandideep and Govindpura industrial areas) creates a fertile testing ground for healthcare diagnostics, biotherapeutics, clean-tech sensors, smart manufacturing, and agritech ventures.

## Why IISER Bhopal for Deep-Tech Incubation

Deep-science startups rarely succeed in generic office spaces; they require wet benches, biosafety hoods, cryogenic analytical instruments, and faculty co-inventors who understand peer-reviewed science. By embedding **10,000 sq ft of incubation space** directly inside the Main Building of IISER Bhopal, IICE compresses the time and capital needed to move a discovery from a university laboratory to a commercial enterprise.
