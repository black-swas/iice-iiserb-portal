## Links to other websites

Some pages link to websites run by other organisations, such as funding agencies and partners. These links are there for your convenience. IICE does not control those websites and is not responsible for what they contain or for their privacy practices. A link does not mean that IICE endorses the organisation or what it publishes.

Links to other websites open in a new tab, and the link says so. Pages on other websites can change or move without notice, so a link may stop working.

## Links to this website

You may link to any page of this website. Please link to the page itself rather than copying its content, and do not show our pages inside a frame on yours, so that visitors can see where the information comes from.
