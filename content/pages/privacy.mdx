This policy explains what happens to the details you give IICE through this website.

## What we collect

When you send a form (contact, pitch, application, mentor, partner or booking), we receive what you type into it, the address of the page you sent it from, and the time. We use it to answer you, to assess applications and requests, and to run IICE's programmes.

We do not ask for more than a form needs, and we do not sell or share your details with advertisers.

## Where it goes

Each submission is saved in a spreadsheet that IICE owns and controls, and is emailed to the IICE office. These steps use Google services, so your submission passes through Google's systems. Only IICE staff who need it to respond can see it.

## How long we keep it

We keep submissions only as long as we need them to respond to you and to keep IICE's records of applications and enquiries.

## Cookies and what stays on your device

This website sets no cookies and has no advertising or tracking code. It does keep a few things in your browser's storage, on your device only: your colour theme, your recent searches, and, if you start the application form, a draft of your answers so you can finish later. You can clear them from your browser settings, and the application form has a button to clear its draft.

Spam protection on the forms uses a hidden field and the time taken to fill the form. It does not identify you.

## Maps and videos

A map or a video is loaded only when you ask for it. It then comes from another service (for example Google Maps or YouTube), which will see your visit and may set its own cookies. The other service's own policy applies.

## Your choices

You can ask to see the details you sent, to correct them, or to have them deleted. Email iice@iiserb.ac.in and say which form you used and the email address you gave.
