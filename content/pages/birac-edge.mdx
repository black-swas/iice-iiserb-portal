The **BIRAC EDGE** initiative at **IICE, IISER Bhopal** was formally announced during **Industry–Academia Conclave 2.0** on **8 August 2026** by **Dr. Debasis Nayak**, Coordinator of the BIRAC-EDGE Program and faculty member in the Department of Biological Sciences at IISER Bhopal.

## Advancing Translational Bioscience at IISER Bhopal

Working in synergy with the **Biotechnology Industry Research Assistance Council (BIRAC)** (a Public Sector Enterprise set up by the Department of Biotechnology, Government of India), IICE supports life-science, biotherapeutics, molecular diagnostics, and medical-device innovators through:

- **Pre-Proposal & Ignition Grant Clinics:** Technical mentoring for innovators preparing applications for **BIRAC BIG (Biotechnology Ignition Grant)**, PACE, SBIRI, and BIPP national calls.
- **Wet-Lab & Bio-Analytical Infrastructure:** Access to IICE molecular biology benches and IISER Bhopal's Central Instrumentation Facility—including confocal laser scanning microscopy, flow cytometry, high-field NMR (500/700 MHz), and UHPLC-HRMS.
- **Clinical & Regulatory Linkages:** Guidance on biosafety clearances, preclinical study design, and intellectual property protection for biological inventions.
