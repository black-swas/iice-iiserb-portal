IICE supports technology ventures at IISER Bhopal from a first idea to the market. The journey has three stages, and a team joins the one that fits where it is today.

Catalyse is pre-incubation, for students, researchers and first-time founders who want to test an idea and reach proof of concept. Culture is incubation, for teams that have a proof of concept and are building a prototype or a product. Commercialise is acceleration, for startups with a product that are looking for market entry, investment and scale.

Current and former faculty, students and alumni of IISER Bhopal can apply, and so can partner universities and startups from outside. Partner universities and outside startups need an IISER Bhopal faculty member as mentor or collaborator.
