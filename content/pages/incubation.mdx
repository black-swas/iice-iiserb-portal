IICE supports science-led and deep-technology ventures at **IISER Bhopal** from an early laboratory result to commercial scale. Whether you are an undergraduate or doctoral student testing a prototype, a faculty member spinning out patented research, or an external startup collaborating with IISER Bhopal, you enter the stage and engagement track that matches your technology readiness level (TRL).

## Three-Stage Venture Architecture

1. **Catalyse (Pre-Incubation · TRL 1–3):** For students, doctoral researchers, and first-time founders who want to validate a scientific hypothesis, conduct customer discovery, and build a working proof of concept with support from the **Innovation Club** and **Startup Studio**.
2. **Culture (Resident & Associate Incubation · TRL 4–6):** For incorporated or incorporating teams with a proof of concept that require wet/dry laboratory space, 2nd Floor Main Building office cubicles, **Central Instrumentation Facility (CIF)** access, IP licensing, and seed-grant facilitation (**DST NIDHI-TBI**, **BIRAC BIG / EDGE**).
3. **Commercialise (Acceleration & Scale · TRL 7–9):** For startups with validated products seeking enterprise pilot deployments, regulatory clearances, public procurement linkages (**MSME RAMP**), and institutional venture capital.

## Eligibility & Faculty Collaboration

Applications are open on a rolling basis to:

- Current and former **faculty members** of IISER Bhopal.
- Enrolled **students and alumni** (BS, BS-MS, Integrated PhD, PhD, and postdoctoral fellows) of IISER Bhopal.
- **R&D partner universities** and research institutes with an active collaboration with IISER Bhopal.
- **External technology startups** registered in India (external applicants and partner-university teams collaborate with an IISER Bhopal faculty member as scientific mentor or co-investigator).
