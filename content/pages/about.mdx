The **Innovation and Incubation Centre for Entrepreneurship (IICE)** is the official Technology Business Incubator (TBI) of the **Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Bhopal**, an Institute of National Importance under the Ministry of Education, Government of India. Operating from **10,000 sq ft of dedicated incubation space** on the 2nd Floor of the Main Building (Administrative Block) at IISER Bhopal's Bhauri campus, IICE was established with grant support from the **NSTEDB Division of the Department of Science and Technology (DST), Government of India**, under the **NIDHI-TBI (National Initiative for Developing and Harnessing Innovations)** scheme.

IICE bridges fundamental scientific research and commercial enterprise. With a strong foundation in biosciences, chemical biology, quantum and functional materials, flexible electronics, and artificial intelligence, IICE supports IISER Bhopal faculty, students, alumni, R&D partner universities, and external deep-tech founders with laboratory space, Central Instrumentation Facility (CIF) access, mentoring, intellectual property protection, and seed-funding facilitation.

## Vision

To be a premier national centre of excellence that catalyses scientific entrepreneurship at IISER Bhopal, forges enduring industry–academia partnerships, and nurtures globally competitive deep-technology ventures for societal and economic impact.

## Mission

1. Build and sustain a vibrant entrepreneurial ecosystem across the IISER Bhopal campus and the central Indian region.
2. Translate indigenous technologies, patents, and scientific discoveries developed at IISER Bhopal into commercial products and services.
3. Empower early-stage startups with wet/dry laboratories, high-end analytical instrumentation, domain mentorship, and investor networks.

## Key Functions & Institutional Mandate

1. **Incubation Infrastructure:** Provide co-working desks, private office cubicles, wet and dry research laboratories, shared office equipment, and technical assistance to resident and associate incubatees.
2. **Structured Venture Mentoring:** Guide startups across three structured stages—**Catalyse** (Pre-incubation), **Culture** (Incubation), and **Commercialise** (Acceleration)—under the oversight of the Managing, Executive, and Technical Evaluation Committees.
3. **Entrepreneurial Skill & E-Cell Activities:** Organize Startup Studio bootcamps, Ministry of MSME Advanced ESDP and RAMP programmes, and student-led **Innovation Club** initiatives.
4. **Regional Ecosystem Leadership:** Stimulate science-based entrepreneurship across Bhopal and Madhya Pradesh in collaboration with national agencies (DST, BIRAC, Ministry of MSME, MP Laghu Udyog Nigam).
5. **Industry–Academia Convergence:** Host flagship forums such as the **Industry–Academia Conclave** to align campus R&D with industrial problem statements.
6. **Alumni & Student Engagement:** Mobilize IISER Bhopal BS, BS-MS, Integrated PhD, and PhD scholars and alumni into startup creation.
7. **IP & Technology Transfer:** Facilitate prior-art searches, patent filing, and commercial licensing of institute-owned intellectual property in coordination with the Office of the Dean, Research and Development (DoRD).

## Funding & Institutional Acknowledgement

IICE gratefully acknowledges the **Department of Science and Technology (DST), Government of India**, whose NSTEDB Division supported its establishment under the **NIDHI-TBI** scheme, and **IISER Bhopal** (Ministry of Education, Government of India) for providing 10,000+ sq ft of furnished incubation infrastructure, laboratory utilities, and Central Instrumentation Facility access.
