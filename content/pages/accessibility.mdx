IICE wants everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use a keyboard, a screen reader, a magnifier or voice control. We build to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA and to the Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW).

## What we do

- Every page can be used with a keyboard alone, and a "Skip to main content" link is the first thing on each page.
- Text can be enlarged to twice its size, and the layout adapts instead of breaking.
- Text and controls have enough contrast, in the light and the dark colour theme.
- Images that carry meaning have a description. Decorative images are hidden from assistive technology.
- Every form field has a visible label, required fields say so in words, and errors are listed at the top of the form and next to the field.
- Animation is off for visitors whose device asks for less motion, and nothing moves without a way to pause it.
- Links that open another website say so.

## Known limits

PDF documents are published as their authors supplied them and may not be fully accessible. If you cannot read one, write to us and we will help you get the information another way.

## Tell us about a barrier

If something on this website is hard to use, email iice@iiserb.ac.in or phone +91 755 269 2384. Tell us the page, what you were trying to do and what got in the way, and the assistive technology you use if you are happy to share it.
