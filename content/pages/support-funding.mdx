IICE has been awarded a grant by the Department of Science and Technology (DST), Government of India, under the NIDHI-TBI scheme. IISER Bhopal has allocated a semi-furnished space of more than 10,000 sq ft, and funds for basic infrastructure and its maintenance. IICE is also keen to seek financial support from other government agencies and from industry.

## Acknowledgement

IICE gratefully acknowledges the Department of Science and Technology (DST), Government of India, whose NSTEDB Division supported its establishment under the NIDHI-TBI scheme, and IISER Bhopal for providing the incubation space and infrastructure.
