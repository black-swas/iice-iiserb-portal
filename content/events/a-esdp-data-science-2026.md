IICE, in association with the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (EECS), IISER Bhopal, organised a one-week Advanced Entrepreneurship and Skill Development Programme (A-ESDP) on "Data Science and Data Analytics" from 23 to 27 February 2026. The Entrepreneurship Skill Development Programme (ESDP) Division, Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises, Government of India, sponsored it.

The programme aimed to strengthen entrepreneurial and technical capabilities in Data Science, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. It drew MSMEs, startup founders, research scholars and students who want to apply data-driven technologies in innovation and enterprise.

## Inaugural session

Dr. Preeti Chandurkar, Deputy CEO, IICE, welcomed participants. Prof. Gobardhan Das, Director, IISER Bhopal, gave the welcome address, and Prof. Chandan Sahi, Dean (Research & Development), introduced the research ecosystem of IISER Bhopal. Dr. Bharathwaj Sathyamoorthy, Coordinator, IICE, described the centre's work to promote entrepreneurship.

## Sessions

Expert sessions covered AI tools, machine learning applications, startup validation, innovation and industry use cases. Participants also visited IICE to see its startup support and incubation facilities. On the final day, Dr. Feroz Khan Suri spoke on financial literacy, startup funding and valuation.

## Valedictory session

Mr. Manish Rajoria, Chairman of Career Society and Director of Aadarsh Technosoft Pvt. Ltd., addressed the valedictory session and encouraged participants to pursue technology-driven entrepreneurship.
