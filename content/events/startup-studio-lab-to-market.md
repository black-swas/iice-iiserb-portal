IICE presented The Start-up Studio webinar on **Lab to Market: paths and opportunities for commercialisation**.

## About the speaker

Ms. Poyni Bhatt is the Chief Executive Officer of SINE, the technology business incubator at IIT Bombay, and a founding member of its team. She is a fellow member of the Institute of Company Secretaries of India and has spent more than a decade helping entrepreneurs and supporting technology business incubation. She has been involved with the startups supported by SINE since 2004 and also mentors startups and innovators outside SINE.

She is a member of several government and trade-association committees on innovation, entrepreneurship and incubation, and sits on the boards of several incubators.
