IICE hosted the MSME MP Laghu Udyog Nigam exposure-cum-orientation programme under the RAMP scheme on 7 May 2026 at IISER Bhopal. MSME MP Laghu Udyog Nigam organised it to give exposure and orientation on incubation ecosystem development and entrepreneurship promotion.

Dr. Preeti Chandurkar, Deputy CEO, IICE, presented on building an incubation centre, startup support mechanisms, innovation ecosystems and institutional entrepreneurship frameworks. About 30 representatives of incubation centres and institutions across Madhya Pradesh took part, including faculty members, incubation coordinators and institutional officials.

Participants also toured the incubation and startup support facilities at IICE, where they saw the incubation infrastructure, the innovation support systems and the startup mentoring mechanisms. Mr. Aman Sinha, Project Incubation Manager, and Ms. Anjali Maran, Project Office Associate, coordinated the programme and looked after participants.

Dr. Abha Rishi, Head of the MP Startup Centre, gave the valedictory address on innovation-led entrepreneurship and the role of institutional incubation ecosystems in startup growth and regional innovation. Certificates were given to all participants.
