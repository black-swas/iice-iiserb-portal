On 11 April 2023, students and faculty members of Truba Institute of Pharmacy, Bhopal, visited the Innovation and Incubation Centre for Entrepreneurship (IICE), IISER Bhopal. The aim was to learn about entrepreneurship and how an incubation centre works.

The students were given an overview of the essentials of entrepreneurship and the startup ecosystem, along with a tour of the incubation centre's facilities. They also visited the institute's Central Instrumentation Facility (CIF), where they learned about the Nuclear Magnetic Resonance (NMR), mass, single-crystal and powder X-ray diffraction (XRD) and Scanning Electron Microscope (SEM) facilities.
