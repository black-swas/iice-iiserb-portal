IISER Bhopal organised Industry–Academia Conclave 2.0 on 8 August 2026, on the theme "Innovate, Collaborate, Translate: Strengthening Industry–Academia Partnerships". The Office of the Dean, Research and Development, and the Innovation and Incubation Centre for Entrepreneurship (IICE), IISER Bhopal, organised it.

The programme was conducted under the leadership of Prof. Raghuvir Singh Tomar (Director In-Charge, IISER Bhopal) and Prof. Sanjit Konar (Deputy Director, IISER Bhopal), with the guidance of Dr. Chandan Sahi (Dean, Research and Development) and Dr. Bharathwaj Sathyamoorthy (Coordinator, IICE). Dr. Preeti Chandurkar (Deputy CEO, IICE) coordinated it.

## Inaugural session

Dr. Abha Rishi (Executive Head, MP Startup Centre, Bhopal) was the Chief Guest, and Mr. Chong Fook Yen (Principal Social Sector Specialist, Asian Development Bank) was the Guest of Honour.

## Technical sessions

- Mr. Rajendra Mootha, Head of New Initiatives at IITM Pravartak, on research ecosystems, industry engagement and innovation.
- Mr. Binu Philip, Senior General Manager and Head of Business Development at Zydus Group, on drug development, technology licensing, business development and commercialisation.
- Prof. Kiran Kondabagil, Department of Biosciences and Bioengineering, IIT Bombay, and Co-founder and Chief Scientific Mentor of Haystack Analytics Pvt. Ltd., on biotechnology, genomics and research translation.
- Mr. Bhavesh Mudgal, Regional Product Head for Startups at HDFC Bank, on the opportunities the banking sector offers startups.

## Panel discussion

The panel on "Strengthening Industry–Academia Partnerships: From Proof of Concept to Successful Commercial Implementation" brought together experts from industry, government, research, MSME and banking. The panellists were Mr. Rajiv Avinash Puri (General Manager, Lupin Limited, Bhopal), Mr. Rajeev Agrawal (Chairman, Association of All Industries, Mandideep), Dr. Manoj Kumar Jain (Senior Advisor and Head, AIGGPA, Bhopal), Dr. Feroz Khan Suri (Manager, BRIC, NII, New Delhi), Mr. Gaurav Goyal (Assistant Director, MSME Indore) and Mr. Ashish Agrawal (Start-up Head, HDFC Bank, Noida). Dr. Bharathwaj Sathyamoorthy moderated the session.

## Startup exhibition

Mr. Jagdish Tanvani, Additional General Manager, Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited, Bhopal, inaugurated the Startup Exhibition.

## BIRAC EDGE Scheme

Dr. Debasis Nayak, Coordinator of the BIRAC-EDGE Program, announced the BIRAC EDGE Scheme at the event.
