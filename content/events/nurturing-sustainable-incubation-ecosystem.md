Dr. Mrutyunjay Suar, Director General (R&D) at KIIT Bhubaneswar and CEO of KIIT-TBI, spoke on "Nurturing a Sustainable Incubation Ecosystem". He shared his experience in innovation, incubation, technology deployment and technology commercialisation.

Faculty members, students and startups incubated at IICE, IISER Bhopal, met Dr. Suar to understand the roadmap to a successful science and technology based enterprise.
