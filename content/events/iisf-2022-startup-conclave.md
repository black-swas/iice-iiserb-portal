IICE took part in the start-up conclave of the 8th India International Science Festival (IISF) 2022, held in Bhopal from 21 to 24 January 2023. It showed technologies and innovations developed with the facilities offered at the incubation centre.

IISF is an annual celebration of innovation in science and technology. It gives incubators and startups a common platform to discuss innovative ideas and to turn them into technologies, products, grants, investment and business opportunities. The Ministry of Science and Technology organises it with the Ministry of Earth Sciences and other scientific ministries and departments of the Government of India, in association with Vijnana Bharati (VIBHA), and the Department of Biotechnology is the lead organiser.

Celebrating IISF 2022 during India's presidency of the G20 gave a further reason to showcase Indian scientific achievements globally. The festival followed the G20 theme "One Earth, One Family, One Future".
