IICE conducted a RAMP MSME exposure session on incubation ecosystem development on 11 May 2026 at IISER Bhopal. The aim was to give practical insight into building incubation ecosystems, institutional entrepreneurship frameworks and startup support mechanisms.

The programme began with a short introduction to its objectives and to why innovation and incubation ecosystems matter in academic and government institutions. Faculty members, incubation coordinators, institutional officials, government representatives and other ecosystem stakeholders from several institutions took part.

Participants visited the Central Instrumentation Facility (CIF), the NMR laboratory and the incubation and startup support facilities at IICE. Dr. Preeti Chandurkar, Deputy CEO, IICE, presented on setting up an incubation centre, startup support services, incubation models, funding opportunities, policy frameworks and government initiatives that support entrepreneurship and innovation. She also covered operating practices and strategies for sustainable incubation ecosystems in institutions.

Mr. Aman Sinha, Project Incubation Manager, and Ms. Anjali Maran, Project Office Associate, coordinated the programme. It ended with a question-and-answer session on setting up an incubation centre, mentorship networks, funding support, institutional sustainability models and strengthening the startup ecosystem.
