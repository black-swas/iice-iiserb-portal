**Xtalsphere Pvt Ltd** (CIN: `U72100MP2025PTC079775`) is a deep-tech materials science spin-off incorporated in 2025 and incubated at IICE, 2nd Floor, Main Building, IISER Bhopal. Mentored by **Dr. Subhajit Roychowdhury** (Department of Chemistry, IISER Bhopal), Xtalsphere bridges India's domestic supply gap in research-grade single crystals for quantum physics, spintronics, and thermoelectric applications.

### Crystal Growth & Material Portfolio

- **Topological & Quantum Single Crystals:** Custom synthesis of magnetic Weyl semimetals ($\text{Co}_3\text{Sn}_2\text{S}_2$), topological crystalline insulators ($\text{Pb}_{1-x}\text{Sn}_x\text{Te}$), giant Rashba semiconductors ($\text{BiTeI}$), altermagnets ($\text{MnTe}$), and 2D van der Waals ferromagnets ($\text{Fe}_3\text{GeTe}_2$).
- **Advanced Growth Techniques:** High-temperature Bridgman growth, self-flux and metallic-flux crystallization, and multi-zone Chemical Vapor Transport (CVT) under controlled inert or vacuum atmospheres.
- **Structural & Transport Verification:** Every crystal batch is validated via Laue back-reflection orientation, powder and single-crystal X-ray diffraction (XRD), and electrical/thermal transport characterization using IISER Bhopal's Central Instrumentation Facility (CIF).
