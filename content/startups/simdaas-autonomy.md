**SimDaas Autonomy Pvt Ltd** (CIN: `U62099UP2023PTC183938`), founded in 2023 by **Sunit Arya** and backed by strategic investment from **MapmyIndia (CE Info Systems)**, is an autonomous-systems software venture incubated with IICE, IISER Bhopal.

### Core Capabilities

- **Simulation-as-a-Service (SimDaaS):** Physics-accurate 3D simulation environments modelled on Indian road geometries, mixed traffic behaviour, and adverse weather conditions.
- **ADAS & Autonomous Vehicle Validation:** Closed-loop virtual testing of perception, sensor-fusion (LiDAR, radar, camera), and path-planning stacks before physical road deployment.
- **Synthetic Dataset Generation:** Automated generation of pixel-annotated multi-sensor datasets for edge-case training of autonomous ground and aerial robots.
