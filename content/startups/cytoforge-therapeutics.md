**Cytoforge Therapeutics Pvt Ltd** is a precision biotherapeutics company headquartered at Cube 6, 2nd Floor, Administrative Building (IICE), IISER Bhopal. The startup develops next-generation cellular immunotherapies and targeted gene-delivery vectors aimed at solid tumors and hematological malignancies.

### Technology Platforms & Pipeline

- **Proprietary Engineering Platforms:**
  - **LentiForge:** High-titer lentiviral vector engineering and bioproduction platform for stable immune-cell transduction.
  - **HelixForge:** Modular chimeric antigen receptor (CAR) construct design and validation workflow.
  - **INViT:** Targeted *in-vivo* immune-cell programming platform designed to reduce manufacturing complexity and cost.
- **Preclinical Oncology Pipeline:**
  - **CFT118:** CD70-targeted CAR-T candidate in preclinical evaluation for renal cell carcinoma (RCC).
  - **CFT286:** *In-vivo* anti-PSCA CAR-Macrophage (CAR-M) candidate targeting solid-tumor microenvironments in pancreatic cancer.
  - **CFT341:** Dual-targeting CAR-T candidate designed to overcome antigen escape in triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC).
