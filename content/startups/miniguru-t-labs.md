**Miniguru T-Labs** (operated by **Miniguru Innovation Pvt Ltd**, CIN: `U85499MP2024PTC073580`), founded in 2024 in Bhopal by **Namita Maithil** and **Pramod Maithil**, is an educational innovation venture incubated at IICE, IISER Bhopal.

### Key Initiatives

- **T-LAB Tinkering Spaces:** Turnkey hands-on tinkering laboratories for schools and community centres equipped with safe mechanical, electronic, and scientific prototyping tools.
- **Project-Based Learning Ecosystem:** Guided maker challenges where children design, build, document, and showcase working science and engineering models rather than memorizing textbook theory.
- **Educator Capacity Building:** Workshops and curriculum modules aligned with National Education Policy (NEP 2020) experiential learning mandates.
