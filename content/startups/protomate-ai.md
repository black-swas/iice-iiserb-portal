**Protomate AI Pvt Ltd** (CIN: `U62013MP2024PTC071169`) is a student-founded artificial intelligence venture incorporated in 2024 and incubated at IICE, 2nd Floor, Main Building, IISER Bhopal. Founded by **Agamdeep Singh** and **Sattwik Kumar Sahu**, the company builds clinically grounded natural-language tools for mental-health accessibility in India.

### Flagship Product — Manomitra

- **Multilingual Clinical Intake Assistant:** *Manomitra* conducts empathetic, medically structured preliminary interviews in Indian languages to identify early behavioural and emotional symptom patterns.
- **Decision-Support for Clinicians:** Generates structured intake summaries aligned with standard psychiatric screening protocols so psychologists and psychiatrists can prioritize high-need patients and reduce diagnostic wait times.
- **Privacy-First Architecture:** Built with strict patient-data anonymization and clinician-in-the-loop safeguards.
