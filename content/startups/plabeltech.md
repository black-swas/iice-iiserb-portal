**Plabeltech Pvt Ltd** (CIN: `U24299MP2018PTC045210`) is a deep-science biotechnology spin-off incubated at IICE, 2nd Floor, Main Building, IISER Bhopal. Founded in 2018 by **Prof. Vishal Rai** (Department of Chemistry, IISER Bhopal) and **Er. Vikas Rai**, alongside senior scientists **Dr. Kamal Vats** and **Dr. Ram K. Mishra**, Plabeltech commercializes chemical biology technologies developed in the Organic and Chemical Biology Laboratory at IISER Bhopal.

### Core Technologies & Products

- **Site-Selective Protein & Antibody Labelling (LDM Platform):** Proprietary Linchpin Directed Modification (LDM) reagents that install single, chemically defined modifications at specific amino-acid residues on native proteins and monoclonal antibodies without genetic engineering.
- **Homogeneous Antibody-Drug Conjugates (ADCs):** Contract research and bioconjugation services enabling controlled drug-to-antibody ratios (DAR) for targeted oncology therapeutics and diagnostic immunoconjugates.
- **Gly-Tag Affinity Purification Resin:** Chemical affinity tag and resin platform for rapid capture and release of native, tag-free proteins in their biologically active state.
- **MS Sensitivity Booster Kits:** Chemical derivatization kits that enhance ionization efficiency and detection sensitivity of low-abundance peptides and proteins in mass spectrometry workflows.
