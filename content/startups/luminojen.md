**Luminojen Pvt Ltd** (CIN: `U62090MP2025PTC074922`) is a research-driven chemical biology and molecular imaging startup incorporated in February 2025 and incubated at IICE, IISER Bhopal. Co-founded by **Prof. Apurba Lal Koner** (Department of Chemistry), **Prof. Gobardhan Das** (Director, IISER Bhopal), **Dhananjaya Sahoo**, and **Naveneet Dubey**, the venture translates indigenous fluorophore chemistry from IISER Bhopal's Bionanotechnology Lab into commercial research reagents.

### Core Offerings

- **Organelle-Specific Fluorescent Trackers:** Photostable, wash-free small-molecule fluorescent probes—including **TD-ER** (a non-cytotoxic Endoplasmic Reticulum marker)—engineered for super-resolution and confocal live-cell microscopy.
- **Custom Fluorophore Synthesis:** Bespoke design and synthesis of solvatochromic, viscosity-sensitive, and near-infrared (NIR) molecular rotors for cell biology and disease diagnostics.
- **Photophysical & Spectroscopy Services:** Specialized contract characterization including steady-state and time-resolved fluorescence lifetime spectroscopy, quantum yield measurements, and live-cell colocalization assays.
