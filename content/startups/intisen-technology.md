**Intisen Technology Pvt Ltd** (CIN: `U33119MP2021PTC058127`) was incorporated in October 2021 at 2nd Floor, Main Building, IISER Bhopal. Co-founded by **Dr. Mitradip Bhattacharjee** (Assistant Professor, Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science, IISER Bhopal, and Head of the Flexible Electronics & Sensor Laboratory / i-Lab) and **Nupur Bhattacharjee**, the venture translates micro-sensor and instrumentation research into field-deployable hardware.

### Focus Areas

- **Flexible & Printed Sensor Systems:** Low-cost, high-sensitivity flexible electronic and electrochemical sensors for physiological monitoring and wearable health diagnostics.
- **Point-of-Care Biomedical Instrumentation:** Portable readout electronics and microfluidic sensor cartridges for rapid clinical biomarker and water-quality detection.
- **Smart Edge Interfacing:** Low-power analog front-ends paired with wireless telemetry for continuous environmental and industrial condition monitoring.
